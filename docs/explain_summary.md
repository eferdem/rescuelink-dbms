# EXPLAIN ANALYZE - before / after indexing

Generated by `sql/06_perf_test.py` on a 74k-row dataset (12,000 batches, ~15,400 allocations, ~21,700 cold-chain checks). Each query is warmed with one execution; the time shown is the median of three measured executions.

| Query | Before (ms) | After (ms) | Change | Plan nodes before | Plan nodes after |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| `at_risk_pipeline` | 4.51 | 1.83 | -59% | Subquery Scan > Hash Join > Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] | Subquery Scan > Hash Join > Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] |
| `donor_leaderboard` | 7.42 | 7.66 | +3% | Subquery Scan > Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] > Seq Scan [organizations] | Subquery Scan > Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] > Seq Scan [organizations] |
| `fill_rate` | 15.80 | 16.07 | +2% | Hash Join > Hash Join > Seq Scan [batch_allocations] > Seq Scan [donation_batches] | Hash Join > Hash Join > Seq Scan [batch_allocations] > Seq Scan [donation_batches] |
| `dormant_donors` | 2.52 | 1.07 | -58% | Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] > Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] | Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] > Seq Scan [organizations] > Index Only Scan [idx_batches_donor_cover] |
| `top_donors_per_district` | 3.55 | 3.52 | -1% | Subquery Scan > Subquery Scan > Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] | Subquery Scan > Subquery Scan > Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] |
| `app_donor_batches` | 0.53 | 0.04 | -92% | Hash Join > Seq Scan [donation_batches] > Seq Scan [food_categories] | Index Scan [idx_batches_donor_cover] > Index Scan [food_categories_pkey] |
| `app_recipient_claims` | 0.59 | 0.15 | -75% | Seq Scan [batch_allocations] > Index Scan [donation_batches_pkey] | Bitmap Heap Scan [batch_allocations] > Bitmap Index Scan [idx_alloc_recipient] > Index Scan [donation_batches_pkey] |

Full plans: `docs/explain_before.txt` and `docs/explain_after.txt`.

Reading the results:

* `at_risk_pipeline` and `dormant_donors` are the selective queries and gain
  the most. The 48-hour pipeline replaces the sequential scan of 15,000+
  allocations with a bitmap index scan on `idx_alloc_recipient` for the
  rolling 7-day window; the batch side stays a sequential scan because 48
  hours is not selective enough. `dormant_donors` turns the batch probe into
  an index-only scan on the covering `idx_batches_donor_cover`.
* `app_donor_batches` and `app_recipient_claims` are the busiest pages and
  switch from sequential scans plus sorts to index scans with an
  already-sorted order, so no sort node remains.
* `donor_leaderboard` and `fill_rate` aggregate every row of the table.
  PostgreSQL correctly keeps the sequential scan: an index would only add
  random I/O. This is a deliberate finding, not a failure - adding an index
  is not automatically an optimisation.
* `top_donors_per_district` reads the whole table as well, so its timing is
  dominated by the aggregate; the observed difference is run-to-run noise
  (repeat `06_perf_test.py` to confirm).
